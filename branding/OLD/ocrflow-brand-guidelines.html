01

The mark

The mark is an aperture — the instant a document is read — opening on one side to let structure flow out to a node. It fuses the two ideas the platform is built on: recognition and flow. It is drawn on a strict geometric grid, with a single open channel and one accent node. Nothing is rounded; nothing is decorative.

02

Lockups & variations

The horizontal lockup is primary. Use the stacked lockup in square or vertically constrained spaces, the wordmark where the mark already appears nearby, and the mark alone for avatars, favicons and app tiles. In the wordmark, OCR is SemiBold and Flow is Regular — separated by weight, never by colour.

04

Typography

Two families, clear roles. Hanken Grotesk carries the voice — headings, the wordmark, body. JetBrains Mono carries the machine — labels, code, data, and anything that speaks in the product's technical register. Both are open-source and free to ship.

Banner & app icon

A repository / social banner and a square app tile, both built from the same geometry. The favicon uses a compact aperture inside a rounded tile — the only place a rounded container is permitted, because the OS masks app icons anyway.
