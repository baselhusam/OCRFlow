01 — LOGOMARK

The Segment mark

An O built from three rounded arcs — the composable stages of a pipeline — broken by gaps where stages connect, with one accent node marking the active stage. It is the heart of the identity and always reads as a single ring.

Three arc stages
Each arc is a swappable stage — detect, recognize, extract.
Composable gaps
The breaks are where stages wire together — modular by design.
Active node
The accent dot is the running stage — the only colored element.

04 — CLEAR SPACE & SIZING

Give it room

Keep clear space of at least one node-diameter (X) on every side. Never place type, edges, or other marks inside that zone.

08 — MISUSE

Don't do this

✕

✕
